Website privacy

apptaztic.com · Last updated 31 October 2026

This page explains what happens to your information when you visit the Apptaztic website, apptaztic.com. It is run by Apptaztic ("we", "us"). If you have a question, email support@apptaztic.com.

Our games have their own privacy policies, which cover what the games do. You can find each one on the game's page, for example PIXEL CONGA.

The short version

  • This website uses no cookies, and no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • There are no accounts, forms or sign-ups.
  • Our web server keeps a short-lived technical log of visits, which includes your IP address.

Cookies

We do not set any cookies or similar technologies on this site, so there is nothing to accept or decline. The pages load nothing from other companies' servers, such as trackers, social media buttons or embedded videos.

Server logs

Like almost every website, when you load a page our web server automatically records technical details of the request: your IP address, the date and time, the page you asked for, the result, your browser and device type, and the page that linked you here, if any.

  • Why: to keep the site secure, to find and fix faults and to block abuse. Our legal basis under the UK and EU GDPR is our legitimate interest in running a secure website.
  • How long: these logs are rotated and deleted automatically after a short period, normally about two weeks.
  • Who sees them: only us and the hosting provider that runs the server. We do not use them to identify or profile individual visitors, and we do not sell them.

If you email us

If you write to support@apptaztic.com, we use your email address and your message only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. We keep it for as long as needed to deal with your question, and then delete it.

Links to other sites

Game pages link to Google Play, and our policies link to Google's pages. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR or the EU GDPR), you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to how we use it. Email us and we will help. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If we change how the website handles information, for example by adding analytics, we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect.
